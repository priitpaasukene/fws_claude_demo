Chicken vs Crocodiles Game

This is chicken vs crocodiles game

During your turn you can:

  • Place egg into empty cell
  • Evolve egg into a chicken

Game Modes:

Play vs Computer: You play as Team Chicken against the computer (Team Crocodile).

Hot Seat Play: Two players take turns on the same device. Player 1 is Team Chicken, Player 2 is Team Crocodile.
