this is chicken vs crocodiles game
during your turn you can:
* place egg into empty cell
* evolve egg into a chicken

Computer takes his turn after your turn.
Computer difficulty levels:
* Random - makes random legal moves
* Easy - wins if possible, otherwise random
* Medium - wins if possible, blocks your wins, otherwise random
* Hard - wins if possible, blocks your wins,
  sets up 2-move wins, otherwise random
